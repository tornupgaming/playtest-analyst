## Write-up (2026-10-26, TUG-121)

**Swept.** 6 configs × the 4 synthetic sessions (TUG-120), one model call per run, 24 runs, $4.44 in total. Each config tests one thing:

| config | changes from v0 | question |
|---|---|---|
| v0 | baseline ([config](../../../analyst/configs/v0.json), [prompt](../../../analyst/prompts/v0.md)) | |
| v0-notelemetry | no telemetry timeline | how much does telemetry add? On the novoice session this is the video-only lower bound. |
| v0-notranscript | no transcript (silent video + telemetry) | how much does the think-aloud add? On the novoice session it is a near-repeat of v0. |
| v1 | [prompt](../../../analyst/prompts/v1.md) adds a "list candidates, then confirm each" pass | does an explicit two-pass procedure in one call find more? |
| v0-rep, v1-rep | none: v0 and v1 run again ([configs](configs/)) | how big is the run-to-run noise? |

fps 2 was not swept: v0 located every plant at fps 1, so denser frames had nothing to recover here, and cost is about two-thirds output tokens anyway.

**Noise is as large as every difference between configs.** v0 and v0-rep had identical inputs and scored 10/12 and 8/12 at strict=category. v1 and v1-rep did the same. No config moved recall by more than 2 of 12, so on this data no config beats another.

**Best on this data: v0, provisionally.** It ties v1 on recall and precision and is simpler and a little cheaper ($1.39 against $1.49 over its two runs). Telemetry and transcript stay in. Without either one, the novoice session lost one plant (the fork without telemetry, the hall without transcript). But v0-rep lost the hall on the same session too, so that evidence is weak.

**Why only provisional.**
- The voice lines are written from the answer key, so the voice runs are optimistic. Every config located all 6 plants on planted-voice (recall any 1.00 in all 6 configs).
- The novoice runs are the honest lower bound: 5 or 6 of 6 located, at 0.67–0.83 recall at strict=category.
- One scripted bot, one route, one level, and 1–2 runs per cell. The clean sessions are 63 s of perfect bot play. Their 0 false positives in 12 runs say little about precision on real, messy play.

**Plants missed often (strict=category).**
- **Plant 5, the fork (confusion): labelled `ui` every time it was found (11 of 12 runs).** It was missed outright once, by v0-notelemetry on novoice. The summaries are right ("the EXIT sign points into a dead end"). The v0 prompt's own `ui` definition covers things in the world that are "misleading", so the model is following the prompt. *Hypothesis:* the answer key's category is the debatable side. Either relabel plant 5 or accept ui|confusion for it before the key is frozen (`frozen_at` is still null; that is Phil's call). A prompt rule written to flip this one case would be fitting to the key.
- **Plant 2, the plaza (ui): always located, labelled `ui` in 6 of 12 runs and `confusion` in the rest.** Same boundary. *Hypothesis:* the prompt's ui/confusion rule ("how information is presented" vs "design of the task or the space") is ambiguous when an objective marker can't be seen. A tie-break rule or examples that don't come from this level might steady it.
- **Plant 3, the hall (confusion): silently missed in 3 of 6 novoice runs** (v0-notranscript, v0-rep and v1-rep produced no hall finding). The strict score hides this: the plaza finding, mislabelled `confusion`, overlaps the hall window through the 10 s padding and is credited as plant 3 (see "recall any" and the plant table). *Hypothesis:* without voice, the only signal is a long dwell with no deaths or idles, which the model must work out from raw timestamps. Giving it per-zone time-in-zone totals computed from the telemetry might make dwell anomalies explicit for any game.

**Eval harness issues found (TUG-102).** (1) With padding, a finding can be credited to the neighbouring zone's plant of the same category, even when the finding names its own zone. (2) One mislabelled finding counts as both a miss and a false positive.

**Cost and latency.** Planted (190 s): $0.22–0.36 per run, 77–153 s of model latency. Clean (63 s): $0.05–0.08, 11–32 s.
